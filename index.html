ABOUT ZANE

Meet the young karateka behind the journey.

What began as something fun at age five has grown into a journey of training, competition, confidence and a dream of one day representing the Philippines.

Hi, I'm Zane.

I am an aspiring karateka from the Philippines, with dreams of one day joining the Philippine Karate National Team and representing our country.

I started karate at age five in July 2023. Since then, every class, belt promotion and tournament has taught me something new. There have been wins, losses, medals and difficult matches, but every experience has helped me grow — and I am still only at the beginning of my journey.

THE NEXT STEP

Back to the national stage — better prepared.

This will not be Zane's first experience on a national stage. Earlier in his journey, he competed at the 6th Karate Pilipinas National Championships, where he reached the second round in both Kata and Kumite but did not advance further. It was a difficult result, but also an important lesson in what national-level competition demands.

Now he has another opportunity. Zane has been selected to represent the Marikina Karate Team at Batang Pinoy 2026 in Bacolod City. This time, he returns with more competition experience, a better understanding of the level he is preparing for, and a clear goal: to arrive properly equipped, ready and confident for both Kata and Kumite.

December 12–18, 2026 Bacolod City Karate · Kata & Kumite
WHAT COMES WITH THE OPPORTUNITY

Preparing for the next step.

Returning to national-level competition also comes with practical needs. For Kata and Kumite, Zane needs the proper approved uniforms and protective equipment required for competition, along with the travel needed to reach Bacolod. We are sharing these costs openly so family, friends and anyone following Zane's journey can understand what it takes to help him arrive prepared. There is no expectation to contribute; this is simply a transparent look at the support surrounding his next chapter.
