2023 THE JOURNEY
BEGAN

From superhero moves at home to real discipline on the mat.

Zane loved Power Rangers and Ninja Kids and would copy their moves around the house. On July 22, 2023, he stepped into his first karate class and discovered something he genuinely loved.

Since then, karate has become a journey of training, competition, confidence and growth—one tournament and one belt at a time.
